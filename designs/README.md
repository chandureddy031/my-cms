# APPLES website: three design directions

One scrolling page, menu from the requirement notes: Home, About Us (vision, mission, objectives, interventions, collaborations, team), Activities, Impact Reports (annual reports for 5 years, audit statements, IT returns, programme reports, financial assistance: FC, Govt, CSR, Local, Individual), Case Studies (live stories, videos), Gallery (photos, videos), Contact (offices, map, feedback form), Donate (UPI QR, bank transfer, pledge).

| Folder | Colour | Fonts | Layout |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-apple-red | White, pale blush, deep red from the logo's "APPLES" | Literata + Public Sans | Centred headline, full-width photo marquee |
| B-graphite | White, light grey, charcoal | Bricolage Grotesque + Atkinson Hyperlegible (easy for elders) | Split photo hero, side-by-side programme cards |
| C-red-soil | White, pale clay, Anantapur red-earth brown | Newsreader + Instrument Sans | Full-width field photo hero, programmes as a list |

Each folder: `01`–`09` one image per section, `00-full-page.png`, `10-components.png` (colour tokens, type scale, buttons, tags, tabs, header, cards, stats, report row, form fields, UPI card, amount picker, marquee), `11`/`12` phone.

Tokens live in `source/tokens.css`. Made with Anthropic's `frontend-design` skill.

Still needed from APPLES (placeholders): UPI ID and QR, bank and FCRA account, FCRA / CSR-1 / 80G numbers, 5 annual reports, audit statements, IT returns, video links, partner logos, team photos, map location.
